Bake & Blast: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Bake & Blast (the "app") uses and how it is handled. The app is published by Velvet Ink Studio ("we", "us"). You can contact us at velvetink49@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no sign-in and no servers run by us. We do not collect your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your progress (scores, coins, settings, unlocked items) is stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's advertising software collects some technical data, described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your high score, coins, game statistics, daily-reward progress, sound settings and the cosmetic items you unlocked. This data stays on your device. We do not receive it. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app offers optional rewarded videos (for example, to earn a few extra coins). These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When ads are loaded or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app automatically collects and shares the following with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes:

DataWhat it is
IP addressMay be used to estimate the general location of the device
User product interactionsApp launches, taps and video views
Diagnostic informationApp launch time, hang rate, energy usage and similar performance data
Device and account identifiersThe Android advertising ID and the app set ID

This data is encrypted in transit. We do not receive it and cannot identify you from it. Google's use of the data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device). We request ads suitable for a general audience (rated G or below).

3. Consent in Europe, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested, so you can choose how your data is used for ads. You can review or change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy choices in the app, where that option is available.

4. In-app coins

Coins are a virtual in-game currency. They have no real-world value and cannot be bought with real money in this version of the app.

5. Sharing, rating and feedback

If you tap Share, Rate us or Feedback, the app hands over to the Android share sheet, Google Play or your email app. We only receive information (such as your email address) if you choose to send us a message.

6. Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

7. Retention and deletion

Because we do not collect or store your personal data, there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app removes all game data from your device. Data collected by Google through AdMob is controlled by Google (see the links above).

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to withdraw consent. We do not hold such data about you, but you are welcome to contact us with any request or question and we will help where we can.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
